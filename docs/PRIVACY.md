# Privacy Policy — Alaik

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

Alaik ("the app", "we") is a wishlist app for events: an owner creates a list of
desired gifts, and guests reserve gifts via a shared link. This policy explains what
data we collect, why, and your choices. By using Alaik you agree to this policy.

> **Publish this at a public URL** (e.g. GitHub Pages, or your website) and enter that
> URL in App Store Connect. Replace the contact email below if you prefer a dedicated
> support address.

## Data we collect

- **Account data** — your email address, and a display name if you provide one. Your
  password is stored only as a salted cryptographic **hash**; we never store it in plain
  text and cannot see it.
- **Content you create** — events (title, type, date) and gift items (title, store,
  price, link, and any image URL you add).
- **Reservations** — which gift a guest reserves. To protect the surprise, an event's
  **owner never sees who reserved which gift** — only aggregate counts.
- **Anonymous guest key** — when a guest reserves without an account, a random key is
  stored **on that guest's own device** so they can cancel their reservation. It is not
  linked to any personal identity.
- **Technical data** — standard server logs (e.g. request time, error diagnostics) kept
  briefly for reliability and security.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, advertising identifiers, or payment
information, and Alaik contains no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

## How we use data

- To provide the core service: authentication, creating lists, sharing links, and
  reserving gifts.
- To notify an event's owner about reservation progress (e.g. 50% / 80% / 100% covered).
- To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We do **not** sell your data or share it for advertising.

## Where data is stored / service providers

App data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by our infrastructure provider
(Railway). Hosting providers process data only to run the service on our behalf. If you
are located in Kazakhstan, personal data is handled in accordance with applicable local
data-protection requirements.

## Data retention and deletion

- You can **delete your account at any time** in the app (Profile → "Удалить аккаунт").
  This permanently removes your account, the events you own, and their gift items.
- Reservations you made on other people's lists are anonymised (unlinked from your
  account) so those hosts' lists are not disrupted.
- Server logs are retained only for a short period.

## Children

Alaik is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Your rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data. Account deletion is available
in-app; for any other request, contact us using the details below.
